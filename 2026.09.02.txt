




Within the XFG.1.1 family, the newly designated XFG.1.1.8, XFG.1.1.10, and XFG.1.1.13 strains are quite interesting. Notably, the XFG.1.1.13 strain is converging toward the S:L441R mutation, one of the defining mutations of the LF.7.9 family, and there are even sub-lineages with additional mutations still awaiting designation.

The descendant of the XFG.1.1.13 strain carrying the S:L441R mutation has been designated as the TL.1 strain.


Within the XFG.23.1.3 family, in addition to the RV.1.5 strain, the RV.2.1.1 strain and its descendant, the TK.1 strain, are highly promising.



Within the RK.1 family, both the RK.1.2.1 and RK.1.3.1 strains are highly promising branches. The RK.1.2.1 strain carries the S:S446N mutation, while the RK.1.3.1 strain carries the S:A1174V mutation.
