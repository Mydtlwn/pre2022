




Within the XFG.1.1 family, the newly designated XFG.1.1.8, XFG.1.1.10, and XFG.1.1.13 strains are quite interesting. Notably, the XFG.1.1.13 strain is converging toward the S:L441R mutation, one of the defining mutations of the LF.7.9 family, and there are even sub-lineages with additional mutations still awaiting designation.
