




Within the XFG.1.1 family, the newly designated XFG.1.1.8, XFG.1.1.10, and XFG.1.1.13 strains are quite interesting. Notably, the XFG.1.1.13 strain is converging toward the S:L441R mutation, one of the defining mutations of the LF.7.9 family, and there are even sub-lineages with additional mutations still awaiting designation.

The descendant of the XFG.1.1.13 strain carrying the S:L441R mutation has been designated as the TL.1 strain.


Within the XFG.23.1.3 family, in addition to the RV.1.5 strain, the RV.2.1.1 strain and its descendant, the TK.1 strain, are highly promising.



Within the RK.1 family, both the RK.1.2.1 and RK.1.3.1 strains are highly promising branches. The RK.1.2.1 strain carries the S:S446N mutation, while the RK.1.3.1 strain carries the S:A1174V mutation.

The reason why the SW.2.1 strain has been able to spread rapidly in the United States and Mexico lies in the combination of the S:S446N and S:N641K mutations.

In addition to the aforementioned strains, within the RK.1 family, the RK.1.5 strain carrying the ORF3a:Q57H mutation is also developing rapidly.



Within the NB.1.8.1 family, apart from the RK.1 family, the PQ.2.8.1 family and the PQ.16.1.1 family show the greatest potential. In the PQ.2.8.1 family, the RC.1.2 strain and the RC.1.4 strain carry the S:A623S and S:D253G mutations, respectively. The S:A623S mutation is also present in the PJ.2.1 strain, while the S:D253G mutation is closely associated with both the PQ.16.1.1 family and the XBB.2 family.


In the PQ.16.1.1 family, both the SV.1.4.1 strain and the SV.5 strain are worth close attention, as they both include strains carrying the S:H445P mutation.

In addition, there is the SV.2.6.1 strain, which carries the S:R346K mutation compared with previous strains.


A new convergent mutation, S:P793S, has emerged in both the XFZ.4.1.2 strain and the SV.2.7 strain. It should be noted that convergent mutations rarely occur in this region, but this particular mutation is extremely unusual and warrants close attention.


Within the RF.5 family, the three extremely important branches are the RF.5.3.1, RF.5.3.2, and RF.5.4.1 strains. Among them, the RF.5.3.1 strain carries the S:V1264L mutation, while the RF.5.4.1 strain carries the N:R204Q mutation. These mutations determine their ability to compete with other strains.



A key and important new recombinant strain, XHE, is an extremely significant and promising branch.
